Common questions

How do I remove the background from a photo?
  1. Open a photo in the editor and choose Cutout.
  2. Tap Remove background. Aperta finds the main subject on your device, so nothing is uploaded.
  3. Choose what goes behind the subject, or keep it transparent.
  4. Save. If you keep a transparent background, Aperta saves a PNG, because JPG cannot store transparency.

Cutout works best when the subject stands out from what is behind it. If the edge looks wrong, try a photo with clearer contrast between the subject and the background.

How do I remove the location from a photo?

Open the Photo details tool and choose the photo. It shows where and how the photo was taken. Turn on Remove location (and, if you like, the option to remove camera and device details), then save a copy. Your original photo is not changed, and the copy is the one to share.

Do this before you send a photo to people you do not know. Anyone who has the original file can see where it was taken.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Aperta is free, and ads pay for it. Before you save or share a finished photo, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline or your ad privacy choice does not allow ads, the export goes ahead without one. Editing never needs an ad.

How do I save a photo to the Files app?

When your photo is ready, tap the share button next to Save to Photos. In the share sheet, choose Save to Files, pick a folder and tap Save. Save to Photos adds the picture to your photo library instead.

I deleted the app and my edits are gone

Aperta keeps no library of your photos, so anything you did not save or export is gone with the app, and we cannot recover it. Your original photos stay in your photo library. A full backup of your device may still contain the app's data if you made one before deleting it.

I saw an ad that seems wrong

Write to us at the address above. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

I want to change my ad or tracking choice

Open Settings in the app and look for the Ad privacy choices row, which appears where the law requires it. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier there.
