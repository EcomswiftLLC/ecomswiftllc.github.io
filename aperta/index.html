A complete photo studio that stays on your phone.

Aperta edits, combines, resizes and compresses your photos on your iPhone or iPad. It is free, there is no account, and every photo is processed on your device.

Your photos never leave your device

Aperta has no account and no server that stores your pictures. Every edit, resize, compression and conversion runs on your phone. We do not run analytics of our own.

  • You pick photos with the iOS photo picker, so Aperta sees only what you choose
  • Saving to your library uses add-only permission
  • Background removal runs on the device
  • No account, no sign-in, no subscription

The only outside service is the ad network. The privacy policy explains what it collects.

Free, and paid for by ads

Aperta has no subscription and no in-app purchase. Ads from Google AdMob pay for it, and they appear in two places.

Where you will see ads

  • A banner on the Home screen.
  • One short rewarded video before you save or share a finished photo. You choose whether to watch it, and one watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes.

When no ad is available

  • The export goes ahead for free.
  • This happens, for example, when you are offline.

Where the law asks for it, the app shows a consent form from Google before personalised ads, and iOS may ask whether the app may track you. Aperta itself runs no analytics, and the only tracking that can happen is through Google's ad software, and only if you allow it. You can say no to the iOS prompt and still use everything. Settings has an Ad privacy choices row where one is required.

Questions

Is Aperta really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no paid version. Ads from Google AdMob pay for the app: a banner on the Home screen, and one rewarded video that you choose to watch before you save or share a finished photo.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Editing, background removal, collage, compress, resize, convert, watermark, ID photos and photo details all run on your device. We never receive your photos.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no sign-up.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Before you save or share a photo, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes, so you can save several photos in a row. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the export goes ahead anyway.

Does it need photo library access?

Not to edit. You pick photos with the iOS photo picker, and Aperta sees only the ones you choose. To save a result, the app asks for add-only permission, which lets it add pictures and does not let it read your library.

What happens if I do not allow tracking?

The app works the same. The ads you see will be less personalised. You can change the choice any time in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking. Aperta itself runs no analytics. If you allow tracking, only Google's ad software may use your advertising identifier for ads.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.

Which file formats can I save?

The editor saves JPG, PNG and HEIC. The Convert tool also makes PDF files from photos.

When can I download it?

Aperta has been submitted to the App Store and is waiting for Apple's review, so there is no download link yet. This page will link to the App Store listing once the app is available.

Who makes Aperta?
